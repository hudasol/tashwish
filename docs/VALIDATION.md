# Validation

Generated by `scripts/make_results_docs.py` from result files in `results/`. Nothing here is typed by hand. There is no ground truth for GNSS interference in public ADS-B data, so every number below is either (a) a controlled test on SYNTHETIC injections into real traffic, (b) a property of the coverage model, or (c) a description of events on real days. Real-day events are evidence of degraded position quality, not proof of interference, and say nothing about who or what caused them.

## 7. Where it fails (derived from the numbers above)

- Jamming-like scenarios of tier `hard` are found less than half the time (0.095).
- Jamming-like scenarios of tier `random` are found less than half the time (0.393).
- Spoofing of one or two aircraft is invisible by design (recall 0.000); at least three aircraft are needed.
- Misses against my own pre-registered predictions: D2, C3, S1, S4, R3, R4, X1, A2.
