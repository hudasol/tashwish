# Validation

Generated by `scripts/make_results_docs.py` from result files in `results/`. Nothing here is typed by hand. There is no ground truth for GNSS interference in public ADS-B data, so every number below is either (a) a controlled test on SYNTHETIC injections into real traffic, (b) a property of the coverage model, or (c) a description of events on real days. Real day events are evidence of degraded position quality, not proof of interference, and say nothing about who or what caused them. Numbers are rounded to three decimals.

> **What this repository shows**
>
> 1. OBSERVED. Study window 2026-02-07 to 2026-03-13 (35 UTC days of public ADS-B). The data change sharply from about 2026-03-02: median aircraft per day 2051 before and 732 after, tested degraded share 0.01 to 0.02 in February and 0.09 to 0.34 from 1 March. No cause is identified or asserted.
> 2. SYNTHETIC. On injected jamming-like scenarios the detector found easy 0.92, medium 0.82 and hard 0.10 (pooled across regimes). By traffic regime, easy was 1.00 on normal days and 0.79 on reduced days; medium 1.00 and 0.56.
> 3. SYNTHETIC. Injected spoofing with 3 or more aircraft was found 0.52 of the time (normal days 0.43, reduced days 0.67) and with 1 or 2 aircraft 0.00.
> 4. OBSERVED. Cells the coverage model called covered lacked enough aircraft 0.058 of the time on normal days and 0.425 on reduced days (pooled across regimes 0.091, which describes neither regime).
> 5. OBSERVED. 27 events were flagged over the 35 days; 23 have an uncertain label and 13 of the 21 spoofing-like or both events involve only the minimum 3 aircraft, so a data error is a live explanation. Events are evidence of degraded position quality, not proof of interference.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SYNTHETIC | A test in which known degradation or displaced positions are injected into real traffic. It measures whether the detector finds what was injected. It is not accuracy against real interference. |
| OBSERVED | A count or rate on real days, with nothing injected. |
| MODEL | A property of the coverage model, the regime labels or the detector settings, checked on real days. |
| ADS-B, GNSS | ADS-B is the broadcast in which aircraft report their own position. GNSS is satellite navigation, the source of that position. |
| NIC, NACp | ADS-B fields in which an aircraft states how good its own position is. Low values count as degraded accuracy here. |
| Cell bucket | One grid cell during one 3 hour UTC bucket. All counts are per cell bucket. |
| Held out day | A day scored once, after the freeze, and never used for fitting or tuning. |
| Development day | A day used to design the system, fit baselines and choose thresholds. |
| Tier | Difficulty class of an injected jamming scenario. Easy: intensity 0.8, 3 or 7 cells, 2 or 3 buckets, high traffic. Medium: intensity 0.5, 3 cells, 2 buckets, high traffic. Hard: intensity 0.3 or 0.5, 1 cell, 1 bucket, mostly low or mid traffic. Random: intensity, area, duration and traffic drawn at random. |
| Intensity | Target degraded share: the share of aircraft in the target cell buckets made to report degraded accuracy (0.3, 0.5 or 0.8). |
| Area | Number of grid cells an injected scenario covers (1, 3 or 7). |
| Duration | Number of consecutive 3 hour buckets an injected scenario lasts (1, 2 or 3). |
| Traffic bin | Median aircraft per target cell bucket: low is 3 to 4, mid is 5 to 9, high is 10 or more, none is a median outside those bins. |
| Recall | The share of injected scenarios that the detector found. Found means at least one event overlaps the scenario in space and time. |
| Traffic regime | Normal when the scored day has at least half of the reference en route aircraft per cell bucket (the reference is the development median); reduced otherwise. |
| Degraded regime | Baseline when the scored day's tested degraded share is below 0.05; elevated otherwise. |
| Tested degraded share | Share of aircraft reporting degraded accuracy among cell buckets with at least 5 aircraft. |
| Covered, enough | A cell hour the coverage model expects to be seen is class covered. Enough means at least 5 aircraft were present. False coverage is a covered unit with fewer than 5 aircraft. |
| Day bootstrap | Interval from resampling whole days, because scenarios and cells within a day share traffic and are not independent. It is the interval to use. |
| Wilson interval | Formula interval for a rate that treats every unit as independent. Shown only where marked and not used as an uncertainty statement for pooled units. |
| HIT, MISS | A preregistered prediction met, or did not meet, the pass criterion written before any held out result existed. |

## 1. Design and discipline

- Study window 2026-02-07 to 2026-03-13 (35 UTC days). Split rule `tashwish-heldout-v1`: 21 development days and 14 held out days, listed in `docs/PREDICTIONS.md` before any held out result existed.
- Frozen at commit `94eafcd93ae903bb84f6f07137ae1b04407e00d0`; held out run executed once (2026-10-12T06:35:22.205861+00:00). Baselines, coverage and the persistent emitter list were fitted on development days only.
- Held out synthetic scenarios: 830 (runs per day 4); development scenarios: 1257. Scenarios within a day share traffic, so intervals marked day bootstrap resample whole days.
- Caveat 1, the regime label uses the scored day's own data. A day is labelled elevated when its own tested degraded share is at least 0.05, and the baseline used for that day is then chosen by the label. Events on an elevated day are therefore measured against a baseline selected using the same day's outcome, which mutes events by construction. The rule and its threshold come from development days only, so this is not tuning on held out days. Read the results on events, R2 to R4 and A3 with this in mind.
- Caveat 2, held out is not the future. The hash split interleaves development and held out days inside a series whose level changes over time (tested degraded share on 1 to 7 March: 0.09, 0.11, 0.13, 0.15, 0.19, 0.25, 0.29). Development days next to a held out day carry its level into the fitted baseline. Held out here means never used for fitting or tuning. It does not mean later in time than the training data, and the days are not independent samples.
- Forward chained check: not done. It would fit on days up to 2026-03-02 and score 2026-03-08 to 2026-03-13. It needs a refit and a second run of the system, and the held out run is single use, so no such number exists in this repository. A forward evaluation on days after the study window is preregistered in `docs/PREDICTIONS_FORWARD.md` and has not been run.

**Events in scenario runs and a precision style number (SYNTHETIC)**

- Scenario runs: 56, each with 12.1 injected scenarios on average. After removing the 33 events whose id is also present in the uninjected run of the same day (real background events), 449 events remain, 8.0 per run.
- Matched events (overlapping an injected scenario) over all remaining events: **0.844** (379/449). This is a share of events in synthetic runs, not a precision for real interference, because the injected truth is known only here.
- Events matching no injected scenario: 70 of 449 (spoofing_like 62, jamming_like 8); of these 66 have medium or high confidence, that is 1.18 per run. Without injection the real rate of medium or high events is 0.50 per day (7 over 14 days).
- Discussion: the unmatched medium or high rate per run is higher than the real background rate, so most unmatched events are not explained by background events that kept their id. Two readings are possible and the data cannot separate them: an injection changes the id or extent of a real event so that the removal by id misses it, or an injection causes a new event outside the scenario footprint. Neither reading has been tested. The matched share is a descriptive number for synthetic runs only.

### Discussion of each miss

- **D2** (OBSERVED; Held out volume resembles the two days I saw). 6 of 14 held out days fall outside the written volume range (2026-02-28, 2026-03-01, 2026-03-03, 2026-03-08, 2026-03-10, 2026-03-13). - **C3** (MODEL; Some false coverage, mostly at low traffic hours). The pooled failure rate 0.091 is below the written band of 0.10 to 0.35, and the slot ordering is the opposite of the prediction (see the row in PREDICTIONS_RESULTS.md). The pooled rate mixes 0.058 on normal days with 0.425 on reduced days, so it is not a typical rate for either. No explanation of the slot ordering was tested.
- **S1** (SYNTHETIC; Spoof jumps with 3 or more aircraft detected). Recall 0.516 is below the 0.80 bar. It rises with the number of displaced aircraft (3 aircraft 0.271, 5 aircraft 0.605, 8 aircraft 0.714), so the minimum cluster size of 3 is where the detector is weakest. - **S4** (SYNTHETIC; Uncertain is a meaningful minority; committed labels are right). The uncertain share 0.330 is above the written upper bound of 0.30 and the committed accuracy 0.754 is below 0.85. - **R3** (OBSERVED; A background rate exists). 3 of 12 against a bar of 4. The held out days contain 10 events in total, so the sample is small and one more day with an event would have met the bar. A background rate of events is therefore not demonstrated at this sample size.
